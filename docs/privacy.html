Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

At Project Hub we respect your privacy. We collect the minimum amount of data possible: your email to sign in, and the tasks you write yourself. We never sell your data or share it for advertising, and the AI assistant only sends the task text to Groq to produce suggestions.

1) Who we are

Project Hub is a task management website (Kanban board, Pomodoro timer, calendar, and analytics), operated and managed by the project owner as an individual. To contact us, use the email: rymaszatemh@gmail.com, or the contact form at the bottom of this page.

2) Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password when you create an account. Accounts are managed through Supabase Auth, and passwords are stored hashed — nobody, including us, can read them.
  • Task data: task titles, status, priority, tag, due date, tracked time, and any subtasks you create.
  • AI assistant: the task title you request help with is sent to Groq for processing (details in section 4).
  • Limited technical data: our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and Supabase may log standard technical information such as browser type and IP address in their operational logs, under their own policies. We use no tracking or advertising cookies; the only cookies are the technical ones needed to keep your session signed in.

3) How we use data

  • Operating the site and verifying your identity when you sign in.
  • Saving your tasks and projects and showing them on any device you sign in from.
  • Providing AI assistant suggestions (breaking down tasks, estimating time, improving titles).
  • Fixing bugs and improving the service in general.

We do not use your data for training, advertising, or sell it to anyone.

4) The AI assistant and Groq

When you press one of the AI assistant buttons, only the task title text is sent to the Groq API, which returns a suggestion (such as breaking the task into subtasks). The provider is Groq Inc., and their privacy policy applies to that processing.

  • Project Hub does not keep a copy of the conversation itself; what we store is only the result (for example, the subtasks you accepted).
  • Groq may retain requests according to their own policies and operational logs; see their policy for details.
Warning: do not put highly sensitive information — passwords, bank card numbers, or health data — into tasks or AI conversations. Content you send is processed by a third party.

5) Third parties we rely on

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (static pages).
  • Supabase — database (Postgres) and account authentication.
  • Groq — processing AI assistant requests.
  • EmailJS — delivering the contact form messages to us.

We currently use no advertising or analytics tools.

6) Security

  • Connections to the site and its services are encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed in Supabase and are never exposed to anyone.
  • Each user's data is protected by Row Level Security, so no user can read or modify another user's data.
  • Communication with Groq happens directly from the browser with a limited-scope key.

That said, no system is 100% secure, and we ask you to use a strong, unique password for this site.

7) Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and data permanently, email us at the address above and your tasks, projects, and account will be deleted from the database within 14 days of the request (usually much sooner). Deleting a single task happens instantly from inside the site itself.

8) Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (all of it is visible inside the site at any time).
  • Correct it (edit any task or project directly in the interface).
  • Delete it (delete tasks one by one, or request full account deletion).
  • Stop using the service and withdraw consent by deleting your account.

9) Children's privacy

The site is intended for general use by people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, and if we discover an account belonging to a child under 13 we will delete it.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time (for example when adding a feature or a new provider). We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page with every material change, and important changes will be announced inside the site.

11) Contact us

For any question or request related to privacy (access, correction, deletion), email rymaszatemh@gmail.com or use the form below — we will reply within a reasonable time.
